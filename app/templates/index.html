About Inkgauge

Inkgauge is a working proof sheet for fiction and nonfiction writers. It calculates readability in pure Python and highlights the five sentences in your chapter that place the heaviest cognitive demand on your reader.

The calculations combine Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, clause complexity, vocabulary polysyllables, and active voice phrasing.

Privacy: Your text is analyzed in memory and not saved. AI features send only the sentence you choose (or the chapter, for Chapter read) to Claude.
